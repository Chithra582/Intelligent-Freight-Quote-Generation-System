# Quote Dossier Generation and History Skill

Generates and archives formal freight quotations.

## Core Capabilities
- Formulates itemized quote breakdowns with 24-hour expiration terms.
- Stores historical quotation dossiers in the searchable shipment registry.
- Emits structured payloads for integration with logistics ERP and TMS systems.
