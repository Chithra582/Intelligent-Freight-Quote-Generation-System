# Dynamic Freight Rate Calculation Skill

Calculates transparent, itemized shipping costs for freight transportation.

## Core Capabilities
- Evaluates base cost per mile according to equipment type and lane density.
- Applies weekly Department of Energy diesel fuel surcharge adjustments.
- Aggregates accessorial fees (liftgate, residential, hazmat) transparently.
